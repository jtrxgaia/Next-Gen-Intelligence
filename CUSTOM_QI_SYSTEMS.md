## A version of QI for your application

EchoQI / QI Works develops purpose-built versions of QI under contract for organizations seeking neuro-symbolic AI integrated into their business, products, or hardware. QI-Works offers custom development and integration of the proprietary EchoQI architecture.

The main engagement is custom development of a QI version that the client owns for its contracted applications. The contract defines the delivered system, ownership rights, retained background technology, any applicable core licenses, and integration and handover scope.

## What the engagement defines

- **Purpose and operating environment:** users, tasks, supported software and hardware, data boundaries, connectivity, and deployment needs.
- **Core and integration work:** reasoning, memory, tools, interfaces, and the application-specific behavior to develop.
- **Ownership and licensing:** the client-owned deliverables and application rights, treatment of existing EchoQI technology, source or binary delivery, and third-party dependencies.
- **Validation:** agreed acceptance tests, execution controls, audit or receipt requirements, recovery behavior, and performance targets to measure.
- **Delivery:** milestones, documentation, handover, and any separately agreed support or maintenance.

A custom engagement is quoted after the application and requirements are understood. Pricing, schedule, exclusivity, certifications, service levels, and support commitments are agreed for the specific contract.
