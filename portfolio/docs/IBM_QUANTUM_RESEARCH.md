# Historical IBM Quantum and Qiskit Integration Research

EchoQI's preserved engineering materials include a Python adapter for IBM Quantum and research workflows for hybrid proposal evaluation.

## Integration capabilities represented in the implementation

- Qiskit and Qiskit Runtime dependency/status checks.
- IBM Quantum Runtime service initialization and backend discovery.
- Explicit backend selection or operational non-simulator backend selection.
- Bell-state circuit construction with measurement.
- Backend-specific transpilation and SamplerV2 job submission.
- Job identifiers, timestamps, shot counts, and measurement-count recording.
- Separate local simulation and classical fallback paths.

## Hybrid research workflows

Preserved upgrade-selection and scoring scripts combine classical readiness, risk, and safety factors with sampled signals. These records document experimentation with quantum-service integration in an AI engineering workflow.

The quantum-inspired cognitive architecture and the IBM hardware adapter are distinct parts of the history: classical graph/reasoning software governs the cognitive runtime, while an external quantum service can supply an explicitly requested experimental contribution.

## Evidence scope

This entry was prepared on October 18, 2026 from inspection of preserved adapter, route, and scoring implementations. It documents implemented integration mechanisms and research intent. It is not a newly executed hardware benchmark or a measured quantum-advantage result.

The toolkit identified in the reviewed source is **Qiskit**. No component named “Quickset” was identified in the inspected integration files.

No IBM job was submitted during this review. Account credentials, instance identifiers, private runtime paths, and proprietary source are excluded from this repository.
